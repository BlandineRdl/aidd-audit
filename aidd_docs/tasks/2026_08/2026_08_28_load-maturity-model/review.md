### The deferred port — contested and upheld

Deciding against `maturity-model.port.ts` is defensible, and `assessment` can be written without reopening it. Checked mechanically: `assessment-composes-never-adapts` forbids `^src/assessment/` → `^src/[^/]+/adapters/`, so `assessment` may never call `loadMaturityModel` itself — but nothing in `.dependency-cruiser.cjs` forbids `cli` → `maturity/adapters/`, and `architecture.md` already names `cli` the composition root that "wires concrete adapters". A `MaturityModel` is a value, not a service: `cli` loads it and passes it into `assess-maturity.usecase` as a parameter. That is a plain argument, not an injected port, and it needs no interface. The deferral is recorded in `architecture.md` with its reason, as the contract required. Upheld with no finding.
